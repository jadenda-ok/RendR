Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

One window, three tools, zero bloat.

Crosshair Studio

Layer-based editor with a live preview on a real game scene. Cross, dot, ring, T-sight, box or your own image — with outlines, glow, rainbow mode and pulse/rotate animations.

Real FPS indicator

Your actual in-game FPS, live in the corner of the overlay. Captured with Intel PresentMon via ETW — no injection, no game-memory access, safe with Roblox anti-cheat.

FastFlags manager

Curated flag library with search and categories, custom flags, bulk import, backup and restore — all through Roblox's own supported settings file. No hacks.

Click-through overlay

Your crosshair renders above Roblox and never blocks your mouse or keyboard. Hotkey toggle, profile cycling, multi-monitor follow, auto-hide when Roblox closes.

Profiles & presets

Create, duplicate and rename profiles. Import/export JSON, export PNG renders, and one-click built-in presets you can reuse forever.

Local & private

No Roblox sign-in, no cloud, no telemetry. Settings and profiles live in a JSON file on your PC. The overlay is a separate window, not a game modification.

Up and running in three steps

1

Download & install

Grab the installer, run it, launch Rendr. Windows SmartScreen may ask — choose More info → Run anyway (the app is unsigned).

2

Pick or design a crosshair

Start from a preset or build your own in the studio. Tweak layers, colors, glow and animations with the live preview.

3

Play

Launch Roblox — the overlay appears on top with your crosshair and real FPS. Toggle it anytime with a hotkey.

Questions, answered

Is it safe with Roblox anti-cheat?

Yes. The overlay is a separate transparent window, and FastFlags use Roblox's own ClientAppSettings.json. FPS capture uses Intel PresentMon (ETW) — no injection, no game-memory access.

Is it free?

Completely. Rendr is open source under the MIT license. No premium tier, no ads, no telemetry.

Why does the FPS indicator ask for admin approval?

Reading frame-present timestamps requires one Windows permission prompt per Roblox session. PresentMon restarts itself elevated, reads the data, and exits when Roblox closes.

Is this affiliated with Roblox?

No. Rendr is an unofficial community tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
